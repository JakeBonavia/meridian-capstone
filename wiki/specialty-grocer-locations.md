# Specialty Grocer Locations

What our ingested sources say about where specialty/fresh-format grocers are opening, with citations — and where they're silent.

_Last updated: 2026-09-21_

## What the sources say

- **Redlands, CA** — Regency Centers is developing a 71,400-sq-ft **Whole Foods**-anchored center. *(Source: John Egan, ICSC Exchange/Commerce + Communities Today, 2026-04-24)*
- **Southern California** — Gelson's (27 traditional SoCal stores) opened small-format **"ReCharge by Gelson's"** micromarkets (3,000–5,400 sq ft) paired with EV fast-charging hubs. *(Source: same ICSC article, 2026-04-24)*
- **Aventura, FL** — Aldi is testing a new modular store format there since September 2025. *(Source: same ICSC article, 2026-04-24)* — note: this wiki categorizes Aldi as a **value** player, not a fresh-format specialty grocer, per JLL's framing (see [industry-context](industry-context.md)); included here for completeness but flagged as not strictly "specialty."
- **National new-store geography (not specialty-specific)** — the Southeast captured 215 of 400 tracked U.S. grocery store openings in 2025, led by Florida, with a secondary Mid-Atlantic cluster (NJ, MD, DC/Baltimore). *(Source: James D Cook et al., JLL Grocery Tracker 2026, 2026-02-26)* — this covers total grocery openings across all formats; JLL does not break it out by specialty vs. value vs. traditional.

## What the sources don't say

Neither source gives a specialty-grocer-specific geographic pattern (e.g., where Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, or Sprouts are concentrating new openings), and neither has direct data on Southern California store-opening activity beyond the two SoCal data points above.

## My own conclusion (not stated by either source)

The specialty-grocer siting evidence available so far is thin and anecdotal — two SoCal projects, no broader pattern. It isn't enough to say whether specialty grocers are targeting Meridian's specific trade areas (LA/Orange/Ventura) more or less than elsewhere. This gap is already captured in [interview-questions](interview-questions.md) (Q15).
