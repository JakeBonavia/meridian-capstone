# Grocery Strategies Shift: New Formats, Store Growth and Investment

## Key claims & data points

**Market size & overall shift**
The U.S. grocery sector is valued at $912.4 billion. Grocers are reinventing formats, identities, and footprints to remain competitive as shopping habits evolve.

**Performance splits among retailers**
JLL's Naveen Jaggi: "The days when any grocery store could anchor a center equally well are fading." Value-oriented and fresh-format concepts now drive growth. Grocery-anchored centers maintain strong performance with below-market vacancy rates and rent premiums, but individual operators perform differently.

**Specialty grocer growth**
Trader Joe's achieved 3% year-over-year growth (period ending Feb. 28), outperforming the entire sector by 6 percentage points. Consumer Edge reports the brand gains traction across income levels and age groups, including double-digit year-over-year growth among Gen Z. Traditional grocers like Publix and Safeway see pullbacks from all income groups, with sharpest declines among lower-income shoppers. Discount grocers (Aldi, Food 4 Less) have plateaued since mid-2025.

Consumer Edge's Michael Gunther: "Shoppers are making more deliberate choices...gravitating toward retailers that give them clear loyalty reasons" whether through value or curated experience. Traditional supermarkets face sustained pressure.

**Target's repositioning**
Target's food-and-beverage segment generated over $24 billion in net sales during fiscal year 2025. The retailer is shifting toward a specialty grocer model, with CMO Cara Sylvester stating the company is "building a truly distinctive grocery destination" featuring emerging brands and wellness products rather than competing as "just another grocer." Target is investing in enhanced in-store food experiences including samplings.

**Major retailers' physical expansion**
- Walmart: remodeling 650+ standard stores and Neighborhood Markets in 2026; opening ~20 stores in 2026-2027. In fiscal 2026, allocated $5.5 billion for 675 remodels and opened 12 stores. Operates 5,200+ U.S. stores as of Jan. 31.
- Costco: targeting 30+ new stores annually; expects 28 net new openings in fiscal 2026 (starting Sept. 1). Operates nearly 640 U.S. stores.

**Aldi's format innovation**
Aldi South Group (7,600 stores globally; 2,400+ in U.S.) began testing a modular store format in September 2025 in Aventura, Florida, continuing through 2026. The redesign introduces flexibility adaptable to various store sizes and urban footprints while maintaining cost discipline. Aldi is opening 150 U.S. stores in 2026 for its 50th anniversary.

**Investment activity**
U.S. grocery-anchored center deal volume totaled nearly $11 billion in 2025, up 42% from 2024 (per JLL's Grocery Tracker 2026). Notable 2026 acquisitions:
- Brixton Capital: $29.4 million (Las Palmas Village, Las Vegas); $100 million (Quad at Whittier, Los Angeles)
- Medipower: $115 million for seven-property portfolio (558,000 sq ft)
- Nuveen: $298 million for seven-property portfolio (956,865 sq ft; six grocery-anchored)
- Blackstone Real Estate: Three portfolios (130 properties) totaling $6.7 billion in 2025-2026
- Regency Centers: Developing 71,400-sq-ft Whole Foods-anchored center in Redlands, California

**Restaurant competition**
Tillster's 2026 Phygital Index Report surveyed 2,144 diners: 29% frequent fast-food less often; 37% frequent fast-casual less often. Meanwhile, 36% visit grocery stores more frequently and 33% visit convenience stores more frequently. Nontraditional foodservice players are winning dining occasions on perceived value.

**Small-format innovation**
Gelson's (27 traditional Southern California stores) opened ReCharge by Gelson's micromarkets (3,000 and 5,400 square feet) pairing premium convenience retailing with Rove EV fast-charging hubs. Amenities include lounge, Wi-Fi, restrooms, and car wash, with inventory spanning fresh produce, hot prepared foods, and grab-and-go items.

## Core driver of change

Market consolidation around differentiation and value propositions — specialty grocers gaining share through curated experiences, discount formats through value, and major retailers through expanded physical presence and remodeling investment.
